# General Information on AWS SAA exam
- **Vendor:** Person Vue (online proctored exam)
- **Passing:**
    - *720/1000 
    - aim to get higher since AWS uses Scaled scoring

- **Exam format**
    - **Duration:** 2 hours and 10 minutes exam time OR 2mins per question
    - **Seat time:** 160 mins seat time (check-in early)
    - **Questions:**
        - total 65 questions
        - 50 scored questions
        - 15 unscored questions
    - no penalty for wrong questions
    - multiple choice, multiple answers

- **Certification**
    - valid for 36 months

- **Study time**
    - 20 to 60 hours
    - aim for 1-2 hours per day over 25-30 days
    - 50%-50 split between video lectures and labs - practice exams
    - recommend studying other tracks in parallel like Developer Associate & SysOps Admins

- **Domains and Weights**
    1. Design Secure Architecture - 30% - 20 Questions
    2. Design Resilient Architecture - 26% - 17 Questions
    3. Design High-Performing Architecture - 24% - 16 Questions
    4. Cost-Optimized Architecture - 20% - 13 Questions
